About me

I’m Awad, a Computer Science student at the University of Nyíregyháza, graduating in 2027. I love data. I’m fascinated by the way a messy pile of numbers can turn into a clear answer, a pattern nobody noticed, or a decision someone can trust.

I’m drawn to the whole journey of data: finding it, cleaning it, structuring it, and making sense of it. Whether I’m studying a job market, organizing information so it’s easy to work with, or just following a hunch in a dataset, I enjoy the process as much as the result.

I’m looking for an opportunity where I can keep learning and put that passion to work.
